Exclude edge cells & minimum cell area

Exclude edge cells drops cells touching the image border, whose geometry is truncated. Minimum cell area keeps labels of at least N px and drops smaller ones — a label of exactly N px is kept, so to drop 3 px labels enter 4. Segmentation can leave a few-pixel gap at a cell vertex that would otherwise be read as a cell. 0 = off.

Advanced options
QC tint
Segmentation QC metrics

Dark = suspicious, bright = fine. Uncoloured cells or edges couldn't be judged by the selected metric. Click a cell to see all its scores.

Junction contrast per edge
Scores every cell–cell edge on its own: how much brighter the edge is than the cytoplasm either side of it. This is the one that catches a boundary drawn where no membrane exists. At or below 0 means the edge is darker than the cytoplasm it separates — which no real membrane can be. Min junction sets the shortest edge worth scoring; it is derived from your image's own junction lengths (25th percentile), so it adapts to resolution. Shorter edges are skipped because a few pixels beside a bright vertex give an unstable reading.
Boundary contrast per cell
The same intensity idea averaged over a cell's whole perimeter. Good for "is this cell's outline generally on the membrane", but one bad edge among six good ones is diluted away — use junction contrast to find a single wrong edge.
Morphology outlier per cell
How unlike the rest of its own frame a cell's area, circularity and compactness are. Flags merged cells (large, ragged) and fragments (tiny, sliver-shaped).
Neighbour count per cell
Distance from the 6 neighbours a well-packed epithelium tends toward. Too many suggests a merge, too few a split. Border cells are skipped — their count is cut off by the field of view, not by segmentation quality.
Measurement failures per cell
Cells whose feature extraction fell back to a sentinel value (failed ellipse fit, zero perimeter, no contour found). These otherwise reach only the debug log, yet still feed PCA and clustering.

Shape descriptors optional

Adds convex-hull, lobing and elliptic-Fourier descriptors per cell, plus local tissue-texture measures. Needed only for the Convex hull, Convolution/lobe, Local tissue texture and Elliptic Fourier families in Classify — skip it if you are going straight to tracking.

Track Cells

Why registration, and why the reference must match

The agreement reference has to share the tracker’s convention. It is computed from mask overlap, so it inherits whatever registration its masks had. Scoring a registered tracking with an unregistered reference — or the reverse — compares two different problems.

It is a property of this movie, not a law

How cells are matched between frames

cost = centroid distance + weight × (1 − IoU) × cell diameter

The mask-overlap term asks how much the two outlines actually overlap. A cell landing on its own previous outline pays nothing; one that doesn't overlap at all pays a full cell diameter. It decides the near-ties where centroid distance has no information left. Centroid only sets the weight to 0; Mask overlap only drops the distance term. Either way the plausible-displacement limit still applies, so a distant cell can't win on a sliver of overlap. Gate scale scales that limit: above 1 loosens it (for movies with large jumps between frames), below 1 tightens it.

Motion compensation predicts each cell forward from its own previous displacement instead of assuming it stays put, then measures from the prediction. It suits a movie with real coherent drift — a translating tissue, or stage drift.

Close 1-frame gaps lets a track that loses its cell for one frame re-attach instead of ending and a new ID being issued. It runs as a second matching pass over whole track segments, after the frame-to-frame pass — the same two-stage structure u-track and TrackMate use. It changes track counts, and therefore cell-loss %.

Re-track a frame range

After hand-correcting segmentation, re-track only the affected frames. Identities outside the range are left alone, and the seam at End+1 is reconciled automatically rather than orphaned.

    Matched, unmatched, un-extracted

    Unmatched = the cell was extracted but has no link in this frame — the tracker did not match it, and a correction can. Un-extracted = Extract never emitted it (minimum cell area, or the border exclusion), so it is not in the cost matrix at all and no correction can reach it; re-run Extract with different settings instead.

    Environment score

    Dark = this cell's neighbours changed a lot between the two frames — worth inspecting for a segmentation or tracking error. Bright = neighbourhood preserved. A low score isn't always an error: a division or delamination lowers it legitimately. Uncoloured cells can't be scored (unmatched, or the track is absent in the compared frame).

    It saturates on small neighbour sets. Two cells each keeping one comparable neighbour, the same one, score a perfect 1.0 — so anything that shrinks the neighbour sets pushes scores up, and the cells that cannot be scored at all leave the result rather than scoring 0. Read the counts line below the image, not the colours alone.

        Census: Correction / Ground-truth Collection

        Label one link at a time — J correct, K wrong, L can’t tell. Hold space to blink.

        How the census works

        The question is always the same: is the cell at frame f the same cell as the one this link points to at f+1? Hold space to blink between the two frames — a correct link shifts slightly, a swap jumps. After K, press 1–3 to pick the right cell or 0 for none of them.

        “Can’t tell” is a first-class answer, not a skip. A link an expert genuinely cannot resolve is a limit of the data, not a tracker error.

        In census mode a few items are checks. Some show a deliberately wrong link; some silently repeat an earlier one. You are not told which, and that is the point — the decoy pass-rate and your own self-consistency are what make the labels quotable. No accuracy can exceed your self-consistency, so it is reported beside every rate.

        The two modes never mix. Ground truth collected against a tracking run is not valid for a run it corrected, so a census session cannot touch the tracking data and a correction session cannot write ground truth — both refusals are enforced in the backend, not by this panel. Correction mode has no decoys (accepting one would inject a deliberate error) and applies every pending fix in one batch with one undo. If you want both, run the census first, then correct.

        What each set selects — and its blind spot

        Every set is a queue, not a score: choosing what to look at judges nothing. Each one is also blind to something, and the blind spots are not independent.

        Every link the overlap reference disagrees with
        The tracker's successor is not the one the greedy best-overlap reference picks. Blind to: anything both agree on, and the reference's answer depends on the registration convention of the masks it is computed on.
        Cells the environment score flags for repair
        Cells whose neighbour set changed most between the two frames (Dice at or below the repair tier). Blind to: it saturates on small neighbour sets — two cells each keeping one comparable neighbour, the same one, score a perfect 1.0, so anything that shrinks the sets pushes scores up and the unscoreable cells leave the result rather than scoring 0. It is also invariant under a coherent permutation of identities, the same blind spot as the reverse-solve set below: those two do not corroborate each other.
        Links the reverse solve disagrees with
        The frame pair is solved again with the frames swapped and the drift negated. A link the forward solve makes but the reverse one does not is listed. Not trivially satisfied: the displacement gate and the overlap term's max(H,V) both come from the row cell, so transposing is a genuinely different problem. Blind to: a systematic but self-consistent mistake — it passes in both directions. It shares that blind spot with the environment score, so these two sets are not two independent pieces of evidence.
        Links the solver was least sure of
        For each assigned row, the gap between its second-smallest and smallest finite cost — the solver's own confidence, free and reference-free. Smallest margin first. Blind to: it rewards any cost that merely spreads values out, and it is comparable only within one cost scale — margins from different cost_form settings cannot be compared.

          Visual inspection of every track

          Each track plays on a loop over its own frames, at the player's fps. ↑ / ↓ walk the track list (< > , . do the same); ← / → step frames inside the current track and pause the loop; Space resumes it.

          K marks the CELL on screen, not the track: what is recorded is (track, frame, cell), so the mark says where the track goes wrong. One track can be marked as many times as it has bad frames.

          Marks are written to GT_.../track_inspection.csv with a provenance file naming the tracking run, the masks and the settings they were judged under — and the tracks you did not reach. Track-level judgements are kept in their own file and are never pooled with the census's link-level verdicts: "I watched this track" and "I judged this link" are not the same evidence.

          Colour
          Cell dynamics

          Solid = you confirmed it. Faded = an unconfirmed candidate. Click a cell to see the evidence and accept or reject it; only confirmed events enter the lineage sheet.

          Division
          A track ends while two adjacent tracks begin beside it, their areas together roughly the mother's.
          Delamination
          A track ends inside the tissue with the cell shrinking and ending small next to its neighbours — a cell that vanishes at full size is a broken track instead.
          Rearrangement (T1)
          Neighbour exchange: two cells lose contact while two others gain it. Each event carries an axis — blue where a contact is newly formed, orange where one is broken. The two axes of one T1 should be parallel: that is how a correctly detected exchange looks, and it is what lets a regional average show the direction of intercalation.

          Filters — each one hides events from the picture; the totals beside them always include everything.

          • Hysteresis: two thresholds and a memory, so a shrinking interface cannot flicker in and out.
          • T1 must hold: how long, in seconds, an exchange must last before it counts as settled. Set the frame interval in Extract to see it in seconds; 0 shows every raw event.
          • Interface length: both the shrinking and the newly formed interface must be at least this long, in pixels of wall. Left empty, the value is read off this movie — the length that best separates flicker from exchanges that hold, on the newly formed side. Microns are shown beside it once Extract has a pixel size. On the newly formed side it mostly removes flicker; on the shrinking side it tends to remove exchanges that did hold, so compare the totals before relying on it.
          • Quartet check: keeps only exchanges whose four cells form a complete ring before and after. It judges the newly formed interface; the broken interface that belongs to a rejected one is hidden along with it.

          Cells at the image border are excluded from delamination before anything else is tested — they left the field of view rather than the tissue.
