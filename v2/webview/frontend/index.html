Segmentation QC metrics

Dark = suspicious, bright = fine. Uncoloured cells or edges couldn't be judged by the selected metric. Click a cell to see all its scores.

Junction contrast per edge
Scores every cell–cell edge on its own: how much brighter the edge is than the cytoplasm either side of it. This is the one that catches a boundary drawn where no membrane exists. At or below 0 means the edge is darker than the cytoplasm it separates — which no real membrane can be. Min junction sets the shortest edge worth scoring; it is derived from your image's own junction lengths (25th percentile), so it adapts to resolution. Shorter edges are skipped because a few pixels beside a bright vertex give an unstable reading.
Boundary contrast per cell
The same intensity idea averaged over a cell's whole perimeter. Good for "is this cell's outline generally on the membrane", but one bad edge among six good ones is diluted away — use junction contrast to find a single wrong edge.
Morphology outlier per cell
How unlike the rest of its own frame a cell's area, circularity and compactness are. Flags merged cells (large, ragged) and fragments (tiny, sliver-shaped).
Neighbour count per cell
Distance from the 6 neighbours a well-packed epithelium tends toward. Too many suggests a merge, too few a split. Border cells are skipped — their count is cut off by the field of view, not by segmentation quality.
Measurement failures per cell
Cells whose feature extraction fell back to a sentinel value (failed ellipse fit, zero perimeter, no contour found). These otherwise reach only the debug log, yet still feed PCA and clustering.

Shape descriptors optional

Adds convex-hull, lobing and elliptic-Fourier descriptors per cell, plus local tissue-texture measures. Needed only for the Convex hull, Convolution/lobe, Local tissue texture and Elliptic Fourier families in Classify — skip it if you are going straight to tracking.

How cells are matched between frames

cost = centroid distance + weight × (1 − IoU) × cell diameter

The mask-overlap term asks how much the two outlines actually overlap. A cell landing on its own previous outline pays nothing; one that doesn't overlap at all pays a full cell diameter. It decides the near-ties where centroid distance has no information left. Centroid only sets the weight to 0; Mask overlap only drops the distance term. Either way the plausible-displacement limit still applies, so a distant cell can't win on a sliver of overlap.

Motion compensation predicts each cell forward from its own previous displacement instead of assuming it stays put, then measures from the prediction. It suits a movie with real coherent drift — a translating tissue, or stage drift.

Close 1-frame gaps lets a track that loses its cell for one frame re-attach instead of ending and a new ID being issued. It runs as a second matching pass over whole track segments, after the frame-to-frame pass — the same two-stage structure u-track and TrackMate use. It changes track counts, and therefore cell-loss %.

Re-track a frame range

After hand-correcting segmentation, re-track only the affected frames. Identities outside the range are left alone, and the seam at End+1 is reconciled automatically rather than orphaned.

          Cell dynamics

          Solid = you confirmed it. Faded = an unconfirmed candidate. Click a cell to see the evidence and accept or reject it; only confirmed events enter the lineage sheet.

          Division
          A track ends while two mutually-adjacent tracks begin beside it, their areas summing to roughly the mother's. Inferred from geometry
          Delamination
          A track ends away from the tissue margin with the cell's area shrinking AND ending small relative to its neighbours. Both terms are needed: a cell that vanishes at full size is a broken track, not a cell leaving the epithelium.
          Rearrangement (T1)
          Neighbour exchange — two cells lose contact while two others gain it. Shown with its orientation axis (blue = bond gained, orange = bond lost); the two reinforce, so a regional mean gives the direction of intercalation. Hysteresis takes two numbers, enter/exit (try 25 / 15): a contact counts once it exceeds enter, stops once it drops below exit, and holds its previous verdict in between, so a shrinking bond cannot flicker. A single hard threshold does the opposite — measured at 20 px it raised the one-frame reversal rate from 21% to 32%, worse than no filter. An exchange that has not settled renders faded, with a dashed axis: on a germband dataset 21% of raw events reverse within one frame. Raise T1 must hold to demand more frames; 0 shows every raw event, which is what TissueMiner counts. Nothing is ever hidden — both totals are shown, because a one-frame flicker may be noise or may be a real reversible exchange, and this cannot tell them apart.

          Cells at the image border are excluded from delamination before anything else is tested — they left the field of view rather than the tissue.
